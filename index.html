WHY THE MODEL MUST BE EVALUATED DIFFERENTLY
The accuracy trap: a model that labels every payment "genuine" is 99.81% accurate yet catches 0% of fraud. This project therefore judges models by recall, precision, PR-AUC and ₹ cost.
